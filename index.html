How an article works

Every article asks a question (Utrum…, "Whether…"), then argues the wrong side first (Videtur quod…, "It seems that…", with each further objection introduced by Praeterea, "Furthermore"). An authority answers them (Sed contra…, "But on the contrary…"), Thomas gives his own answer (Respondeo dicendum quod…, "I answer that it must be said that…"), and he replies to each objection in turn (Ad primum ergo dicendum quod…, "To the first, therefore, it must be said that…").

Latin text: the Leonine edition (Rome, 1888) as published by the Corpus Thomisticum (corpusthomisticum.org). Vocabulary, notes, analyses, and translations are original.
