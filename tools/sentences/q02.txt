# Question 2: whether God exists.

= Primo, utrum Deum esse sit per se notum.
@ I q. 2 pr.
> First, whether God's existence is self-evident.
! Deum esse, "that God exists" (literally "God to be"), is the subject of sit; per se notum, "known through itself", means self-evident.
