Lessons

Short lessons on the grammar you need to read the Summa. They assume you know the noun declensions, adjective agreement, the indicative of verbs, and the rest of Latin for the New Millennium, Chapters 1–10. Each lesson teaches something the Summa needs beyond that, with examples from Questions 1–10, and ends with practice. The lessons build on each other, so take them in order the first time.

Latin text: the Leonine edition (Rome, 1888) as published by the Corpus Thomisticum (corpusthomisticum.org). Lessons, notes, and translations are original.
